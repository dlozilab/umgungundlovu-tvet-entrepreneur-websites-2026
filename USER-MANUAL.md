# Your Business Website
## A friendly guide to managing your site

Welcome! This guide shows you how to sign in, update your business information, add photos, and check what customers see. You do not need to know how to code.

> **Your goal:** Keep your website useful, accurate, and welcoming. Work through one section at a time, save your changes, then preview the site.

## Before you begin

Have these ready if you plan to update your site:

- The email address and password supplied for your website admin account.
- Your current business name, description, services, contact information, and trading hours.
- Good-quality photos saved on your phone or computer.
- Accurate registration information and B-BBEE details, if you want to display them.

Use your own account. Do not share your password with customers or include passwords in screenshots.

## 1. Start here: sign in

1. Open your business website in a browser.
2. Go to the admin sign-in page by adding `/login` to the end of the website address. For example: `https://your-site-address/login`.
3. Enter your **Email Address** and **Password**.
4. Select **Sign In**.
5. When sign-in succeeds, the admin area opens.

If you do not have an account, or cannot remember your password, contact mLab. The sign-in page asks you to phone mLab for a password reset.

> **Screenshot idea:** Show the sign-in form with the password hidden. Use a demo account or blur the email address. Never show a password.

## 2. Understand your dashboard

The **Dashboard** is your starting point after sign-in. It shows the website sections that are complete and those that may need attention.

- Select a dashboard item to open that section.
- **Done** means the dashboard considers that section complete.
- **Needs attention** means something may be missing. Select the item to review it.
- Select **View your site** to open the public website.

The dashboard is a helpful checklist, not a guarantee that every detail is correct. Preview the public site after making updates.

### Admin menu

Use the admin menu to move between:

| Menu item | What you can manage |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | Website setup checklist |
| Business details | Business name, headline, and short description |
| Branding | Logo, brand colour, and share picture |
| About | Owner photo and business story |
| Services | Up to three service listings |
| Gallery | Hero photos, gallery names, and work photos |
| Contact and hours | Phone, WhatsApp, email, address, hours, and customer options |
| Compliance | Registration information and optional badges |
| Help | Short answers to common questions |

On a computer, the admin menu is shown beside the page. On a phone, use the menu button near the top of the screen to open the navigation. Selecting a page closes the mobile menu.

> **Screenshot idea:** Capture the dashboard and menu together on a computer. Take a separate mobile screenshot with the menu open.

## 3. Make and save changes

Most text changes are **not saved until you select _Save Changes_**.

1. Open the section you want to update.
2. Edit the fields.
3. Review your spelling and details.
4. Select **Save Changes**.
5. Wait for the save message before leaving the page.

The save button is disabled when there are no unsaved changes or while a save is in progress. If you have edited text and move to another page before saving, your edits may be lost.

### Photo updates work differently

When you replace a photo, the website uploads and saves it as part of the photo action. Wait for the upload to finish and for the new preview to appear before navigating away.

Branding images and the About photo also save when the upload finishes. The brand colour, text fields, and gallery names still need **Save Changes**.

## 4. Update business name and introduction

1. Open **Business details**.
2. Update the fields:
   - **Business** — the name shown on your site.
   - **Headline** — the large sentence near the top of the home page.
   - **Short Description** — a brief introduction. This is also used when someone shares your link.
3. Select **Save Changes**.
4. Select **View site** to check how the information looks.

### Writing a strong introduction

- Keep the headline short and easy to understand.
- Use the short description to say what you do and where you serve customers.
- Use everyday language. A customer should understand the offer at a glance.
- Check names, spelling, and punctuation before saving.

> **Screenshot idea:** Show the three fields with friendly example text, then show how the headline appears on the public home page.

## 5. Set up your branding

Open **Branding** to manage your logo, colour, and the picture shown when your website link is shared.

### Change your logo

1. Find **Logo**.
2. Select **Replace**.
3. Choose an image from your device.
4. Wait for the upload to finish.
5. Check the preview and the logo in the site header.

The logo appears in the menu bar, footer, and browser tab icon. A clear image with the logo easy to see works best.

### Choose your brand colour

1. Find **Your colour**.
2. Select the colour swatch or open the colour picker.
3. Choose your business colour.
4. Check the colour preview and **Button preview**.
5. Select **Save Changes**.

The rest of the site keeps dark text on a light background for readability. Your selected colour is used for buttons and smaller details.

### Set your share picture

The share picture is the image people may see when your link is sent in apps such as WhatsApp or Facebook.

- Select **Replace** under **Share picture** to upload an image you already have.
- Select **Make one for me** to create one from the hero photo and logo currently on your website.
- Wait for the upload or generation to finish, then check the preview.

If the generated picture does not look right, upload a different image instead. A share picture can take time to appear in messaging apps because those apps may keep an older preview.

> **Screenshot idea:** Capture the logo preview, colour picker, and share-picture preview. Use a demo logo if the business does not want its branding included in a public manual.

## 6. Tell your story

1. Open **About**.
2. Under **Owner photo**, select **Replace** and choose a clear photo of the owner.
3. Wait for the image preview.
4. Write your story in **Your story**. You could explain how the business started, what you are known for, and what matters to you.
5. Select **Save Changes**.
6. Select **View site** and check the About section.

Keep the story personal, truthful, and focused on what customers should know. Break up long paragraphs if the field allows it.

> **Screenshot idea:** Show the owner-photo preview and story field. Get permission before using a person's photograph in a manual shared outside the business.

## 7. Add or update services

The site can show up to three services.

1. Open **Services**.
2. For each service you want to show:
   - Enter a clear **Name**.
   - Add a concise **Description**.
   - Select **Replace** to add or change its photo.
3. Select **Save Changes** to save text edits.
4. Wait for photo uploads to finish.
5. Preview the Services section on the public site.

Leave a service name blank if you do not want that service displayed. Use a different photo for each service where possible. Describe the service in terms customers understand, and avoid promises you cannot guarantee.

> **Screenshot idea:** Capture one completed service card in the admin and the matching card on the public site.

## 8. Manage your photos and galleries

Open **Gallery** to update the large hero images and the categorized work galleries.

### Customize hero captions

The hero can show different text for each slide.

1. In **Gallery**, find **Hero captions**.
2. Under **First hero slide text** or **Second hero slide text**, enter a **Hero headline** and **Supporting text**.
3. Select **Save hero text** for that slide and wait for the confirmation.
4. Select **View site** to preview the slide text.

If either field is left blank, the site uses the **Headline** or **Short Description** from **Business details** for that field. The second slide's text is used when the second hero photo is present.

### Update the hero photo

The **Hero photo** is the main photo at the top of your website. Select **Replace**, choose a photo, and wait for the upload to finish. A second hero slide is optional and can be removed with **Remove**.

Choose a photo that represents the business clearly. Check the crop and visibility on a phone as well as a computer.

### Rename or organize work galleries

The **Our Work galleries** area lets you manage up to six categories such as Delivery, Installation, or Custom Projects. Each category can have up to four photos (24 gallery-photo slots in total).

- Edit a **Gallery name** to change its label.
- Select **Add gallery** to create a category, then give it a name. Once six galleries exist, the button is disabled. Remove an existing gallery if you need to make room for a different one.
- Select **Remove gallery** to remove a category from the gallery list.
- Select **Save Changes** to save the gallery names and list.

Every gallery needs a name before saving. If you see a message asking you to name a gallery, fill in any blank names and try again.

### Add photos to a gallery

1. Find the gallery you want to update.
2. Select **Replace** for a photo slot.
3. Choose a photo from your device.
4. Wait for the upload and preview.
5. Repeat for additional photos.

You can add up to four project photos to each named gallery. The first photo is used as the gallery card's cover image. Select **Remove** to clear a gallery photo when that option is available.

The page also has slots for **Premises**, **Work**, and **Delivery** photos. Optional second photos can be removed. The Hero photo is the main photo at the top; the second hero slide is optional.

### Choose good photos

- Use clear, well-lit photos that you have permission to publish.
- Show real work, products, staff, or premises relevant to the business.
- Avoid photos with private information, visible customer details, or people who have not agreed to be shown.
- Use a descriptive image where possible; avoid uploading an unrelated image just to fill a space.

> **Screenshot idea:** Show gallery names and photo slots in the admin, followed by the public gallery cards. A second screenshot can show a gallery open with its image navigation.

## 9. Update contact information and hours

1. Open **Contact and hours**.
2. Update the information customers need:
   - **Phone number**
   - **WhatsApp number**
   - **Email address**
   - **Address**
   - **Trading hours**
3. Select **We deliver** if the business offers delivery.
4. Select **We take walk-ins** if customers can visit without an appointment.
5. Select **Save Changes**.
6. On the public site, test each contact option.

### Contact information tips

- Check every digit in your phone and WhatsApp numbers.
- Use an email address the business checks regularly.
- Enter a location customers can find. Selecting the address on the site opens directions.
- State closed days clearly in your trading hours.
- Test the WhatsApp button using a device that can open WhatsApp. If a number begins with a South African `0`, the site formats it for the WhatsApp link.

> **Screenshot idea:** Show the contact form, then the public contact section with phone, email, address, and WhatsApp options visible. Use approved business contact details only.

## 10. Add business registration details

1. Open **Compliance**.
2. Add any details you want shown:
   - **Registered name**
   - **CIPC registration number**
   - **Year established**
   - **South African owned business** badge
   - **B-BBEE level**
3. Select **Save Changes**.
4. Preview the lower part of your public site.

Only select badges and enter registration information that are accurate and current. If a field does not apply or you do not want it shown, leave it blank or leave the option unselected.

> **Screenshot idea:** Show the compliance form with sample values, not actual registration numbers, unless the business has approved their publication.

## 11. Preview your website

Select **View site** in the admin header to open the public website. You can also use your normal website address.

Review the site from top to bottom:

1. **Home:** Is the business name, headline, and hero photo right?
2. **About:** Does the story read well? Is the photo appropriate?
3. **Services:** Are the names, descriptions, and photos current?
4. **Gallery:** Do the gallery names and photos make sense?
5. **Contact:** Are all links and opening hours correct?
6. **Footer:** Are displayed registration details and badges accurate?

Check on both a phone and a computer. On smaller screens the site uses a menu button for navigation, and the sticky WhatsApp contact icon may appear near the bottom of the screen. The WhatsApp action in the contact section takes its place while that section is visible.

If you make changes, return to the admin page and save them, then refresh the public site to review the latest version.

## 12. Sign out safely

When you finish:

1. Open the admin menu if you are using a phone.
2. Select **Sign out**.
3. Close the browser if you are using a shared device.

Signing out helps prevent someone else from making changes using your open session.

## 13. Troubleshooting

### I cannot sign in

- Check that the email address is the one associated with your admin account.
- Check that the password is entered correctly.
- If you have forgotten your password, phone mLab for a reset.
- If you are sure the details are correct but sign-in still fails, contact mLab.

### The admin page says it could not load the site

Check your internet connection and reload the page. If the problem continues, contact mLab and tell them which page you were opening and what message appeared. Do not send your password.

### My text changes are missing

Text edits need **Save Changes**. Return to the relevant section, make the changes again, select **Save Changes**, and wait for the confirmation.

### My photo is still uploading or does not appear

- Keep the page open until the upload finishes.
- Check that the device has a stable internet connection.
- Try choosing the photo again.
- If the preview says it is unavailable after uploading, contact mLab and include the page name and a screenshot of the message.

### A gallery will not save

Give each gallery a name before selecting **Save Changes**. A blank gallery name prevents the gallery list from saving.

### My website link still shows an old share picture

Confirm the right image is saved under **Branding**. Messaging and social apps may keep a cached preview of a link, so the change may not appear immediately in an old conversation.

### I cannot see the WhatsApp button

The floating WhatsApp icon is intended for smaller screens and may not appear on a computer-sized browser. On a phone, scroll away from the contact section; when the WhatsApp action in the contact section is visible, the floating icon hides to avoid showing two contact buttons.

### Something else looks wrong

Contact mLab. Include the page you were on, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and a screenshot if possible. Hide private details before sending it.
