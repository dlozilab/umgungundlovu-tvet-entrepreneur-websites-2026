Help

Short instructions for the things you will do most often.

How do I change a photo?

Open Gallery, find the photo you want to change, and press Replace. Choose a new photo from your phone.

How do I change my trading hours?

Open Contact and hours, type the new hours, and press Save changes.

Why does my link look plain when I send it?

Open Branding and set a share picture. That is the photo people see when you send your link on WhatsApp.

Why only one colour?

All the writing on your site is black on white so that it is easy to read. Your colour is used for the buttons and the small details.
